Good to know

Questions, answered.

Are the prices really the same?

Yes. You pay the same rates you'd find booking the hotel directly or through any major site — there's no markup. The good stuff is funded by IMPT, not added to your bill.

How does the carbon offset work?

Every confirmed booking retires one tonne of verified CO₂ as IMPT carbon credits — real, traceable climate projects. It's built in and paid by IMPT.

Does it cost me anything extra?

No. Booking here is free and the same price. You also get a €5 welcome credit toward your first stay.

Who is IMPT?

IMPT is a hotel-booking and carbon-offset platform operating across 195 countries — putting real climate action into everyday travel and spending.
